Site Feedback

How to install

  1. Drag the orange button above to your bookmarks bar
  2. Navigate to the website you want to review
  3. Click the "Site Feedback" bookmark in your toolbar
  4. A feedback toolbar will appear — sign in with your email
  5. Start pinning comments!
Note: If you don't see a bookmarks bar, press Ctrl+Shift+B (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+B (Mac) to show it. The bookmarklet works on most websites but may be blocked by sites with strict security policies (e.g., banking sites).
